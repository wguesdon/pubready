The association between Gene A (a.u.) and Gene B (a.u.) was assessed with a Pearson correlation (n = 60). The method was chosen from the Shapiro-Wilk normality of each variable. The correlation was r = 0.71 (P < 0.001), with a linear fit and 95% confidence band shown. Significance was set at P < 0.05. Analyses were performed in Python 3.10.12 with pingouin 0.6.1; the figure was produced with seaborn 0.13.2.
