A heatmap of 30 features across 20 samples was drawn with ComplexHeatmap. Values were z-scored across samples within each feature (row scaling). Rows and columns were hierarchically clustered. A per-feature Welch t-test (control vs treated), BH-adjusted was applied per feature; 20 feature(s) were significant (adjusted P < 0.05) and are marked with stars. Rendered in R 4.4.1 with ComplexHeatmap 2.22.0 and circlize 0.4.16.
