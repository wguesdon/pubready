Expression (a.u.) was compared across 4 groups with a one-way ANOVA (F(3, 36) = 235.83, P = 1e-23). The effect size was generalized eta-squared = 0.95. Pairwise comparisons used Tukey's HSD; only comparisons reaching significance (adjusted P < 0.05) are annotated on the figure. Analyses were performed in R 4.4.1 with rstatix 0.7.2; figures were produced with ggpubr 0.6.0 and ggprism 1.0.5. Group sizes: het (n = 10), ko (n = 10), rescue (n = 10), wt (n = 10).
