Rate (nmol/min/mg) was modelled as a function of Substrate (uM) with the Michaelis-Menten equation v = Vmax S / (Km + S), fitted by nonlinear least squares to each level of inhibitor (3 curves) (n = 72 points). The starting values were the largest rate for Vmax and, for Km, the substrate concentration whose mean rate is nearest half of it. Each 95% confidence interval is the estimate plus or minus t(0.975, n - 2) times its asymptotic standard error. Large points show the mean +/- SEM of the replicates at each concentration, and small points show each replicate. A fit was reported as not determined when Vmax or Km was not positive, when Km lay above the highest substrate concentration, or when the 95% CI of Km or of Vmax held 0. 0 uM: Vmax = 119 (95% CI 116 to 123), Km = 18.5 (95% CI 16.5 to 20.5), R2 = 0.991; 2 uM: Vmax = 121 (95% CI 118 to 124), Km = 27.7 (95% CI 25.3 to 30.1), R2 = 0.996; 10 uM: Vmax = 121 (95% CI 116 to 126), Km = 65.1 (95% CI 58.4 to 71.9), R2 = 0.995. Extra-sum-of-squares F tests compared the separate fits with one shared Km (a Vmax for each curve) and with one shared Vmax (a Km for each curve): shared Km F(2, 66) = 151.84, P = 2.0e-25; shared Vmax F(2, 66) = 0.39, P = 0.68. Fitted in R 4.4.1 with stats::nls and drawn with ggplot2 3.5.1.
