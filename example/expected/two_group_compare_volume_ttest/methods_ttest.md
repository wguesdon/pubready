Tumor volume (mm^3) was compared between the two groups with a Student's two-sample t-test. Normality was assessed with the Shapiro-Wilk test. Equal variances were assumed. Effect size is reported as Cohen's d. Significance was set at P < 0.05. Analyses were performed in R 4.4.1 with rstatix 0.7.2; figures were produced with ggpubr 0.6.0 and ggprism 1.0.5. Group sizes were n = 12 and n = 12.
