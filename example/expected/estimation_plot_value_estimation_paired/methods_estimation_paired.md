Marker (a.u.) was analysed with estimation statistics, with pre as the reference group (n = 22). For each group the paired mean difference against pre (group minus reference) is reported with a 95% bias-corrected and accelerated (BCa) bootstrap confidence interval from 5000 resamples. The resamples were drawn with the Park-Miller minimal standard generator (multiplier 48271, modulus 2^31 - 1, seed 20261029, restarted for each comparison), each resample drawing the subject indices of the pairs as floor(u n). The bias correction is the normal quantile of the proportion of bootstrap differences strictly below the estimate, the acceleration comes from the jackknife of the mean difference over every pair, and the interval ends are type-7 quantiles. Hedges' g is Cohen's d over the average standard deviation of the two conditions with the exact small-sample correction. A paired t-test gives each P value. post: mean difference -11.2 (95% CI -13.3 to -9.02), Hedges' g = -0.67, t(21) = -9.97, P = 2e-09. Computed in R 4.4.1 and drawn with ggplot2 3.5.1.
