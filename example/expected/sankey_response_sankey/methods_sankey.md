The state response of 80 patients (patient) was followed across 3 visits of week (8, 16, 24). Visits are in numeric order, and states in the order set by the analyst (complete response, partial response, stable disease, progressive disease). The diagram shows 108 transitions between consecutive visits; a patient missing at a visit takes no part in the transitions into or out of it, and the transitions table gives every count. The diagram is descriptive, and no test was applied because more than two visits. Drawn in R 4.4.1 with ggplot2 3.5.1.
