Expression (a.u.) was compared across 4 groups with a one-way ANOVA (F(3, 36) = 235.83, P = 1e-23). The effect size was partial eta-squared = 0.95. Pairwise comparisons used Tukey's HSD; only comparisons reaching significance (adjusted P < 0.05) are annotated on the figure. Analyses were performed in Python 3.10.12 with pingouin 0.6.1 and scikit-posthocs 0.17.0; figures were produced with seaborn 0.13.2. Group sizes: het (n = 10), ko (n = 10), rescue (n = 10), wt (n = 10).
