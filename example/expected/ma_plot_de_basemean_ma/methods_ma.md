Differential-expression results for 6000 genes were shown as an MA plot of the log2 fold change (M) against the mean expression (A, log10 mean of normalized counts). Genes with padj <= 0.05 and |log2 fold change| >= 1 were called significant: 176 were up-regulated, 171 down-regulated and 5653 not significant. 76 genes without a p value were counted as not significant. The 15 significant genes with the smallest p are labelled. The differential test itself was performed upstream; this figure summarises its output. Drawn in R 4.4.1 with ggplot2 3.5.1 and ggrepel 0.9.6.
