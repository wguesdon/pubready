Viability (%) was modelled as a function of Concentration (uM) with a four-parameter logistic curve (variable Hill slope) fitted by nonlinear least squares on log10 dose (n = 72 points, 3 curve(s)). The 95% confidence interval of the midpoint was computed on the log10 scale from the standard error of the fit and back-transformed. cmpd_A: IC50 = 0.045 (95% CI 0.0348 to 0.0581, Hill slope -1.08, R2 = 0.988); cmpd_B: IC50 = 0.3 (95% CI 0.235 to 0.382, Hill slope -1.07, R2 = 0.987); cmpd_C: IC50 = 1.63 (95% CI 0.88 to 3.02, Hill slope -0.89, R2 = 0.978). An extra-sum-of-squares F test compared one shared IC50 with one IC50 per curve, with the shared fit profiled over the midpoint and each Hill slope held between 0.05 and 20 in magnitude: F(2, 60) = 97.82, P = 1.3e-19. Fitted in R 4.4.1 with stats::nls and drawn with ggplot2 3.5.1.
