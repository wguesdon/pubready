Differential-expression results for 1200 features were shown as a volcano plot. Features with |log2 fold change| >= 1 and padj <= 0.05 were called significant; 75 were up-regulated and 75 down-regulated. The 15 significant features with the largest product of |log2 fold change| and -log10 padj are labelled. The differential test itself was performed upstream; this figure summarises its output. Drawn in R 4.4.1 with ggplot2 3.5.1 and ggrepel 0.9.6.
