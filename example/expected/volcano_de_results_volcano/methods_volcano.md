Differential-expression results for 1200 features were shown as a volcano plot. Features with |log2 fold change| >= 1 and p <= 0.05 were called significant; 75 were up-regulated and 75 down-regulated. The differential test itself was performed upstream; this figure summarises its output. Drawn in R 4.4.1 with ggplot2 3.5.1 and ggrepel 0.9.6.
