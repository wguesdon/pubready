Response (a.u.) was analyzed by a 2-way ANOVA (Type II sums of squares) with factors genotype, treatment. Effects tested: genotype (F(1, 42) = 67.34, P < 0.001); treatment (F(2, 42) = 22.61, P < 0.001); genotype x treatment (F(2, 42) = 39.40, P < 0.001). Significance was set at P < 0.05. Analyses were performed in R 4.4.1 with rstatix 0.7.2; figures were produced with ggplot2 3.5.1.
