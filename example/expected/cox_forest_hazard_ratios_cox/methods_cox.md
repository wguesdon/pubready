Hazard ratios were estimated with a Cox proportional-hazards model (120 subjects, 75 events) including arm, age, sex, stage. Significant predictors: armtreated (HR = 0.54, 95% CI 0.34-0.88, P = 0.013). The proportional-hazards assumption was not violated (global cox.zph P = 0.58). Analyses were performed in R 4.4.1 with survival 3.6.4, and the forest plot was drawn with survminer 0.4.9 (tidied with broom 1.0.7).
