Tumor volume (mm^3) was compared between the two groups with a Student's two-sample t-test. Normality was assessed with the Shapiro-Wilk test. Equal variances were assumed. Effect size is reported as Cohen's d = 4.11. Significance was set at P < 0.05. Analyses were performed in Python 3.10.12 with pingouin 0.6.1; figures were produced with seaborn 0.13.2 and statannotations 0.7.2. Group sizes were n = 12 and n = 12.
